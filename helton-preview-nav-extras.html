If you only read one tab besides Overview, read this — why Helton preview says Beacon, and why body blocks are not frame sections.

Beacon in Live Preview, not on the live site

Helton DB and bake contain zero “Beacon Insurance Group.” Tenant name is Helton. Live Preview does not use the bake. buildPreviewSite() in sectionFixtures.ts tries siteSchema.safeParse(intake). On failure it merges intake onto previewSite, whose branded fixture is Beacon (name, about, team, reviews, stats, owner quote). Unsupplied or Zod-stripped fields stay Beacon. That is the bug WO-495 fixes.

Frames vs body

GSE today edits frame sections (tenants.frameOverrides): Header, RelatedCoverages, ReviewStripSection. Article body blocks (prose, FAQ, reviewStrip block, figure) live on pages.body per page. Google reviews in the article after FAQ is a body block extra, not the frame. Related Coverages stays a frame section. Do not move the coverage grid into the article in this work.

Template C

Template C uses SidebarShell (left rail ~17rem). FrameComposer skips the top Header on C. Helton has ~55 products, so nested <details> in the rail is unusable. The flyout must overlay the page. A/B still use top Header.

Correction Channel today

CorrectionChannel is a centered paragraph under the whole page in PageRenderer. Mailto from correctionChannelMailto (correctionsEmail or business.email). Footer already has Privacy, Terms, Editorial Process, Trust & Standards, AI Info as trustNav. Compact Credit does not include the channel.

Why not overlay Beacon as a schema fallback?

Helton preview then shows another agency’s name, about, team, and reviews. Preview stops being a check against the live site. Empty structural placeholders plus a fallback notice are honest; Beacon copy is not.

Why not write extras onto each product page.body?

Helton has ~55 products. Writing would clobber Unique Articles and duplicate on re-save. Compose at bake from tenant JSON. LPE and generation never copy extras into pages.body.

Why skipBuild on extras approve?

GSE-007.3 queues a rebuild of affected pages. For extras that is every product page. Frame structure already uses skipBuild; Update Site still publishes. Same path for __bodyDefaults__.

Why not client JS for the mega-menu?

Static export. No-JS is an invariant (AC-NDT-007.6). Hover/focus-within/details only.
